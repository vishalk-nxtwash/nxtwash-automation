Washbooks create a timing difference between when you receive cash and when you earn revenue
Step 1: Sale — Customer pays $60 for a 10-wash book. You receive cash, but you haven't earned it yet. This is a liability (deferred revenue).Step 2: Redemption — Customer uses a wash($6 per wash). Now you've earned that revenue. Liability decreases, revenue increases.Step 3: Expiration — Unused washes expire. The remaining liability becomes "breakage revenue" — you keep the cash with no service obligation.
The Formula: We subtract sales (removes unearned cash from revenue) and add redemptions + expiration (adds earned revenue). This converts your cash-basis daily sales into accrual-basis net revenue.
